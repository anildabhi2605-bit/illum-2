Security Disclosure — Plaintext Local Storage

API keys are stored in plaintext inside your local .env file on your computer. They are never transmitted to external telemetry or third parties, but they are NOT encrypted at rest on your hard drive. Ensure your local workstation is protected, and never commit or share your .env file.
